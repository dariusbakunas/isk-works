# ISK Works name and brand assets

The ISK Works **code** is open source under the [AGPL](LICENSE). The **name** and the **brand assets**
are not part of that license. This page explains what you can do with them.

Brand assets are:

- the name "ISK Works" and the beaver astronaut mascot
- `web/iskworks-web/src/assets/mascot*.png`, `web/iskworks-web/src/assets/help/`,
  `web/iskworks-web/public/*.png` and `docs/images/`

The images were generated with Midjourney. The maintainer reserves whatever rights they hold in them;
this page does not claim more than that.

## You may

- **Run ISK Works with the assets as they ship**, including self-hosted instances, modified builds you
  run for yourself, your corporation or your alliance, and the published Docker images.
- **Refer to the project by name**, for example "a fork of ISK Works", "runs on ISK Works" or
  "compatible with ISK Works", in blog posts, videos, guides and links.
- **Show the assets when talking about the project**, such as in screenshots, reviews and tutorials.

## Please don't

- **Use the name or the mascot for a different product or for a fork you distribute to others.**
  If you publish a fork, as a public repository, published images or a hosted service for people
  outside your own group, give it its own name and replace the brand assets.
- **Use them in a way that suggests the ISK Works project endorses you**, or that your instance is the
  official one when it isn't.
- **Put them on merchandise** or use them in advertising for something else.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether your use is fine, open an issue and ask. Most reasonable uses that help
people find or run ISK Works will be fine.

*This page states the project's expectations; it is not legal advice.*
